Surah al-Baqarah 2:255

اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ ۗ لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ

God, there is no deity but He, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all. Neither drowsiness overtakes Him nor sleep.

The commentators agree that this clause states God's exclusive claim to divinity, and they differ over whether القيوم names the one who sustains others or the one who subsists without support. Al-Tabari reports both readings from the early authorities; al-Razi argues the two are not in conflict.
