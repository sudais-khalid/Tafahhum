Surah al-Baqarah, verse 255

The Verse of the Throne

God, there is no deity but He, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all. Neither drowsiness overtakes Him nor sleep.

Saheeh International

اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ

Part 1 of 3 اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ

There is no deity but He

Fourteen commentators discuss this phrase. They agree on what it establishes and differ on one word, which is set out below.

Al-Tabari died 923 CE Earliest of the three

He means by this: God, besides whom no object of worship deserves worship, and He is the Living who does not die.
